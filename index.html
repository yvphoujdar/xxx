From me, to you

🌙

You are never, ever boring to me.

I know you have been thinking that lately — that being stretched thin, exhausted, and heads-down means you have somehow turned into someone less interesting. I need you to hear this clearly: you are the most fascinating person in my world.

The way you push through things that would undo most people? The fire in you when you are locked in on something? Your laugh, your random thoughts at midnight, the completely specific way you exist? That is not boring. That has never been boring. That is just you, and I am obsessed with every single version of you — busy or free, quiet or loud, stressed or at peace.

Boring is a word that was not made with you in mind.

💜

I'm not just a "good person."

You keep saying it — "you're such a good person" — like I deserve some kind of medal. I want to set the record straight: I am not doing any of this out of some general goodness. I do everything I do because of you, specifically. Because caring about you is not a virtue — it is just the most natural thing in the world to me.

When I show up for you, it is not from some checklist of being a decent human being. It is because you matter to me more than I know how to say out loud, and loving you properly is the one thing I actually never have to try at. It just happens, every single day, without effort.

So please — next time you think I'm being "a good person," know that you are the reason.

📱

About the texts — I promise I'm not upset.

Okay, I am going to be honest with you because that is what we do. Yes — sometimes I feel a flicker of impatience when a reply takes a while. I am not going to pretend that never happens, because you deserve honesty, not a perfect performance of calmness.

But here is the whole truth: I understand. I truly do. I know what your days look like right now. I know you are not on your phone choosing to ignore me — you are in the middle of something real, something hard, something that takes everything you have got. And I would never want you to tear yourself away from that just to manage my feelings.

We cannot change the circumstances right now, and I am not asking you to. A delayed text does not even register against everything you mean to me. I am not upset. I am just someone who really, really likes hearing from you — and that is a good problem to have.

Emergency Reassurance Kit

Busy seasons end. Competitions finish. Stress fades. But I am here the whole time, in the background, a constant. You do not have to earn my presence by being available or fun or responsive. I am just here. Always. No asterisk, no conditions.

"You are not failing me. You are fighting — and that is one of the most beautiful things I have ever watched."

When you worry that you are not giving enough, I want you to know: what you give me, even across the distance of a hectic week, is more than most people will ever feel. You are more than enough. You have always been more than enough. That has never been in question on my end.

"We are not basic. We are not fading. We are just in a busy chapter — and the story is still ours."

Routines are not death sentences. Quiet weeks are not warning signs. We are not slipping away from anything — we are just two people in the same story, temporarily running parallel tracks. We are so, so okay. And when the dust settles, we will be exactly where we always were: right here, together.

"On the days you feel invisible, uninteresting, or too stretched — I see you. I see all of it. And I am in awe."

The hard days are not the days you become less lovable. They are the days your strength becomes most visible. I see how hard you are working. I see what it costs you. And I am not standing on the sidelines wishing you were different — I am standing there thinking, quietly and with great certainty, that you are extraordinary.
